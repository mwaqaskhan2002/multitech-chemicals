Frequently Asked Questions

Multi PU Coat is a premium, single-component, liquid-applied polyurethane waterproofing membrane. Upon contact with atmospheric moisture, it cures to form a highly elastic, seamless, and fully adhered elastomeric membrane. It is specifically formulated to withstand pooling water, extreme UV exposure, and thermal cycling without degrading.

Yes, Multi PU-Roof Coat forms a durable, wear-resistant layer suitable for light to medium foot traffic. For heavy industrial traffic areas, a topcoat or protective wear layer can be integrated during application.

Unlike water-based acrylic coatings that re-emulsify under standing water, polyurethane membranes remain completely impermeable to ponding water and offer significantly higher tensile strength and elasticity.

When applied according to manufacturer guidelines, Multi PU-Roof Coat provides long-lasting protection with a service life exceeding 15 to 25 years.

Execution Protocol

Precision application ensures maximum longevity. Our certified technicians follow a strict protocol for substrate preparation and coating application.

1
Surface Prep

High-pressure washing and mechanical grinding to remove laitance, dirt, and previous failing coatings to ensure optimal adhesion.

2
Priming

Application of a specialized epoxy or polyurethane primer to seal concrete pores and create a strong chemical bond for the topcoat.

3
Base Coat & Detail

First thick layer application. Special attention is given to upstands, drains, and pipe penetrations with reinforcing geotextile fabric.

4
Top Coat Finish

Final UV-stable aliphatic polyurethane layer applied to provide the bright white, reflective finish and ultimate weather resistance.
